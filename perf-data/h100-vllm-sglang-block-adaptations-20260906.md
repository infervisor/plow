## Promotion and kill rules

Keep existing numerical thresholds unchanged: fixed operator checks, exact quant bytes/scales where required; full raw/centered1% + argmax gate. No mask except declared suppressed logits, no NaN/Inf acceptance, no reduced-row subset. E1/E4 remain held until fixed gates pass.

Proposed performance screening policy (not a measured promise): two alternating orders minimum, isolated GPU, same clocks/graph mode, kernel+merge/epilogue included, fresh outputs, SASS/register/spill/arena evidence. Primitive requires explicit >L2 eviction outside timed kernel or a documented cold ring; block_run is warmed block ranking and does not provide isolated-kernel eviction. Do not silently compare these regimes.

Quick screen only relevant2 contexts1K/32K and M1/16 before full20-cell block grid. Advance at most two candidates when target block median improves>=5% in both orders, no covered guard cell regresses>3%, all quality passes. Kill/rework when gain is within run variation, hidden launch/merge/zero costs erase it, new spills cause block regression, or any quality/capacity/missing-row gate fails. Batch-specific accepted cells may become compiler-selected arms; never claim all-M success.

Meaningful serving-win target: after block success, run one short existing vLLM bench serve finalist screen, then full matched matrix only for surviving candidates. Seek>=15% output-throughput margin over fresh vLLM with no TTFT/TPOT/p99ITL regression beyond noise on declared workload scope; require reversed order and confidence intervals separated from parity before saying 'beats vLLM'. Current campaign is far from that condition. Single-block gains cannot prove end-to-end or mixed-arrival scheduler benefit.

CPU verification:14 pinned official files fetched, current local kernel/compiler/mux/harness paths inspected; no GPU, production edits, builds or promotion. Artifact `matrix.json` enumerates requested cells and blocked Qwen cases; `commands.json` preserves existing runnable narrow controls and broader non-runnable templates explicitly.
