Actual Gemma12 projection shapes gained roughly 9–12% from higher grid concurrency; cp.async depth6 was mostly parity at the best grid. This does not establish equivalent interpreter occupancy.

- E1 tensor-core QK+PV passes standalone numerical gates. It remains slower than the actual library references (FA4 for Gemma HD512, FA3 for Qwen HD256). Split-count selection improves coverage, but posthoc selection is not a validated packet planner. Broad-interpreter integration and full-model gates are next.
- E3 four-stage skinny GEMM passed 2,560 correctness rows and 1,008 timing rows, including zero, split-K compute, and finalize. Posthoc median speedups over native scalar M4/8/16/32 are 1.54/2.02/3.57/6.34×; versus cuBLASLt they are 0.955/0.941/0.939/0.874×. Held-repeat and held-shape results are retained separately. No M1 measurement, fused GLU/QKV gate, local reduce packet integration, or serving qualification yet.
- E4 HD512 four-arm comparison passed all 64 full rows. WG64 stage1 is 2.98–3.45× faster than the deployed standalone body, but still 1.61–3.48× FA4 across tested Gemma cells. Same NS3 and merge included. The actual role needs separate arena/resource and full-model gates; a standalone kernel result is not TTFT.
