# gfx950 BF16 GemmWide c8 qualification

Date: 2026-09-04. GPU: MI355X. Toolchain: ROCm 7.14.0 from `nix develop`.

## Result

For `M=8192,N=1536,K=7168`, the existing 128x384x64 c8 body is the qualified
single-op winner. Its full-network gate improves TTFT but is E2E-neutral; the compiler
experiment remains default-off.

| body | tile | median | p10 | p90 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| tagged `GemmWide` c8 | 128x384x64 | 164.398 us | 159.850 us | 173.220 us |
| `GemmWide` c2 | 128x256x64 | 223.929 us | 219.014 us | 225.044 us |

c8 is 59.532 us / 26.6% faster than c2. The other current BF16 rungs are slower:
`Gemm` 240.639 us, `GemmC5` 247.542 us, `GemmMed` 256.614 us, and `GemmSmall`
413.999 us. The c8 launch is the exact 64x4 = 256-workgroup grid.

At `M=4096,N=1536,K=7168`, c2 remains the winner (124.7 us vs c8 143.8 us), so
the c8 selector also requires an exact qualified shape and exact 256-workgroup grid.

## Correctness and resources

- Full BF16 output: bit-exact c8 vs c2.
- Standalone c8: wave64, 512-thread workgroup, VGPR 192, SGPR 50, no spills/private,
  LDS 147456 B.
- Paired full interpreter: control/c8 text 640128/653952 B. Both report VGPR 256,
  SGPR 108, AGPR 0, occupancy 2, VGPR spill 8, SGPR spill 78, private 1348 B,
  LDS 147504 B. The body adds 13824 B of text but no object-wide resource cliff.
- Interpreter-equivalent ordered A/B at M8192: control 311/306 us vs c8 232/236 us.
  At M4096 the unselected path stayed at parity: control 164/162 us vs candidate
  162/164 us.

## Provenance and dispatch gate

The current records use dense-family digest `gfx950-870078e93f2c92f0`, oracle
`gemm-f64-dot-spotcheck-v1`, and campaign `k3-mi355x-current-c8`. c8 has TuneDB
measurement id 32862, distinct from live device opcode 94, and maps to
`(GemmWide, i7=0x00800180, BM=128, BN=384, BK=64)`. This prevents c8 timings from
being mistaken for the c2 body sharing the opcode.

The opt-in is `--emit-gemm-wide-c8-shape MxNxK`. Selection additionally requires
gfx950 BF16, divisible tile geometry, exactly 256 workgroups, a qualified/current c8
record, and c8 being the fastest measured candidate. With the option absent, the
64-byte wire packet remains exactly the pre-c8 c2 packet.

The tagged decision records the same one measured TuneDB lookup as an ordinary tile
decision. The six-program full-graph regression holds both control and c8 candidate at
7650/7650 measured decisions; 324 packets gain only the explicit c8 tag.
